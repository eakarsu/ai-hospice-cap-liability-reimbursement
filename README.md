# Hospice Cap Liability & Reimbursement

Forecast aggregate and inpatient cap exposure, validate beneficiary allocation, and reconcile repayment or reimbursement amounts.

**Primary buyer:** Hospice providers and health systems. **Evidence:** beneficiary elections, service days, payments, transfers, live discharges, inpatient days, sequestration, cap calculations, notices, and settlements.

Full local application built with React, Vite, Express, PostgreSQL, and OpenRouter. Includes 15 domain-specific capabilities, 105 custom AI workbench fields, three scenario-fill controls per feature, operational registers, workflow transitions, analytics, professional AI decision briefs, audit history, and at least 15 PostgreSQL records per capability.

## Domain capabilities

- Election benefit registry
- Beneficiary count calculation
- Service-day ingestion
- Transfer allocation
- Live-discharge treatment
- Payment ingestion
- Aggregate cap calculation
- Proportional method
- Streamlined method
- Inpatient day ratio
- Sequestration treatment
- Notice reconciliation
- Liability forecast
- Settlement payment workflow
- Provider cap analytics

Run `./start.sh`, then open <http://127.0.0.1:4654>. API: `5654`.

Administrator: `runtime-admin@example.com` / `LocalDemo!2026`. Operator and reviewer credential buttons are available on the login page.
